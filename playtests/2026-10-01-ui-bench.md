# UI bench, 2026-10-01 — mid-session surfaces

Not a playtest. A deliberate pass over the in-play UI with state driven straight
into the engine, to look at the panels rather than the fiction. Ran on a throwaway
campaign ("UI test bench", id 3) so the real one was never touched.

## What held up

- Scene panel, backdrop art (`tavern-5.webp` chosen from the scene text), character
  panel with HP bar, AC, the six ability boxes, hit dice and slot pips, and the
  attack list off the sheet.
- `add_creature` with `class_name: ranger` gave STR/DEX saves — the fix from the
  Hallowmere session is holding.
- The commoner fallback: Corvin Hask with no scores came out 4/4, AC 10.
- NPC portrait resolved to the stock library file and rendered beside the name.
- Vendor stock built with `give_item` + price, three lines, correct quantities.

## Finding 1 — the browser follows the wrong campaign after a resume (fixed)

`readTable` draws whatever `mostRecentCampaign()` returns, which is `listCampaigns()[0]`,
ordered by `updated_at`. `updated_at` is bumped in exactly one place: `appendStep`.
`load_campaign` only assigns `#campaign` in memory.

So resuming an older campaign left every panel — scene, character sheet, cast —
showing the *previously* played campaign until the first mutating tool call landed.
That window is the whole start of a session: the recap, the "last time you were…",
the first look at the sheet.

Reproduced live: engine on campaign 1, browser drawing campaign 3 with its character
and its cast.

Fixed by having `load_campaign` touch the campaign, since loading *is* playing.
`persistence.test.ts` pins it, and fails without the change.

## Finding 2 — "which campaign is loaded" is per-process, and nothing says so

There is no persisted current-campaign flag; each `Store` holds `#campaign` in its own
memory. Three processes can therefore disagree at once, which is what happened here:
my engine on campaign 3, the DM's engine still on campaign 2, the browser drawing
whichever row was written last.

In ordinary single-player use the DM is the only writer, so this stays invisible —
but it is only ever one extra writer away from surfacing, and the symptom is baffling
when it does. Finding 1's fix narrows it; it does not remove it.

Worth noting what the DM did when the ground moved: it called `get_state` and
`list_campaigns`, said plainly that the engine did not back up the premise, laid out
the three options, and refused to switch campaigns on its own initiative. That is the
prompt working exactly as intended under genuinely confusing conditions.

## Not reached

`offer_choice` and `open_shop` render from the WebSocket tool-result stream, not from
`/api/state`, so neither can be exercised by driving the engine out of band — they
need a live DM turn. Still untested.
